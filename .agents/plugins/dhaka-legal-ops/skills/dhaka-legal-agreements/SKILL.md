# Dhaka Legal Agreements & Tenancy Compliance Skill

Use this skill whenever generating, reviewing, or explaining rental tenancy deeds, lease terms, advance deposits, or municipal property tax rules in Dhaka City.

## 1. Governing Legal Framework
- **Statutes**: *Premises Rent Control Act, 1991 (বাড়ি ভাড়া নিয়ন্ত্রণ আইন, ১৯৯১)* and *Transfer of Property Act, 1882*.
- **Stamp Duty**: Standard residential and commercial tenancy agreements in Bangladesh are executed on a **৳ 300 Non-Judicial Stamp** (নন-জুডিশিয়াল স্ট্যাম্প), signed by First Party (Landlord), Second Party (Tenant), and at least two witnesses.
- **Registration**: Leases exceeding 12 months technically require sub-registry registration under the Registration Act; most Dhaka agreements use an **11-month or 12-month renewable term** to stay streamlined.

## 2. Standard Clauses & Dhaka Market Norms
1. **Rent Payment Window**: Payable monthly in BDT (`৳`) on or before the **10th of each English calendar month** via bank transfer, bKash/Nagad, or crossed cheque.
2. **Security Advance Deposit**:
   - **Residential (`home`)**: Typically **2 to 3 months** of rent (up to 4 months in Gulshan/Banani luxury units). Refundable upon vacant handover minus verified utility arrears or structural damage.
   - **Commercial (`office` / `shop`)**: Typically **3 to 6 months** of rent (or higher for prime ground-floor retail showrooms).
3. **Mandatory Termination Notice**: Standard **2-month (60 days)** written notice required from either party prior to vacating or terminating the lease.
4. **Utilities & Service Charges**:
   - Electricity (**DESCO** in North Dhaka / **DPDC** in South Dhaka), Water (**Dhaka WASA**), Gas (**Titas Gas** / LPG), and Building Service Charge (lift, security, common generator, cleaning) are borne separately by the Tenant unless explicitly stated as inclusive.
5. **Rent Escalation**: Typically reviewed every **2 years** (e.g., 5%–10% mutual adjustment) rather than arbitrary mid-term hikes.

## 3. Residential vs. Commercial Special Requirements
- **Residential (`home`)**:
  - **DMP CIMS Registration**: Under Dhaka Metropolitan Police (DMP) directives, landlords must collect NID copies and submit the **Citizen Information Management System (CIMS)** tenant verification form to the local Thana.
  - **Bachelor vs. Family**: Explicitly state occupancy type and visitor/society rules if applicable.
- **Commercial (`office` / `shop`)**:
  - **NBR VAT & TDS**: Corporate tenants are often required by the National Board of Revenue (NBR) to deduct **TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)** and remit applicable VAT on commercial rent.
  - **Trade License NOC**: Landlord must provide the building holding number, rent receipt, and No Objection Certificate (NOC) so the tenant can obtain a **DNCC** or **DSCC** Trade License.

## 4. Mandatory Disclaimer
Always include a brief note clarifying that the generated agreement is a standardized template for convenience and does not constitute formal legal representation; parties should verify final execution on a ৳ 300 Non-Judicial Stamp with witnesses.
