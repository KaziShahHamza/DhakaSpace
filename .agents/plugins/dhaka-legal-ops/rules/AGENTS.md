# DhakaSpace Legal & Marketing Ops Rules

1. **Bangladesh Tenancy Compliance & Disclaimers**:
   - All generated tenancy agreements and holding tax calculations must reference *Bangladesh Premises Rent Control Act, 1991* and include a clear disclaimer that outputs are standardized templates/estimates, not formal legal representation.
   - Always include **DMP CIMS** (Dhaka Metropolitan Police Citizen Information Management System) tenant registration reminders for residential leases.
   - Commercial leases (`office`, `shop`) must note NBR VAT/TDS obligations and DNCC/DSCC Trade License NOC requirements.

2. **Bilingual Copywriting Standards**:
   - All monetary values must strictly use BDT (`৳`) and area in `sqft` (never USD or square meters unless explicitly requested).
   - Bengali marketing copy must use natural, idiomatic Dhaka real-estate terminology (e.g., তিতাস গ্যাস, লিফট, জেনারেটর, সার্ভিস চার্জ, দক্ষিণমুখী).
   - Never fabricate amenities (e.g., do not claim "Titas pipeline gas" or "2 parking slots" unless provided in the listing input).
