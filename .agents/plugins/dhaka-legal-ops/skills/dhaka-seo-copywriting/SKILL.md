# Dhaka Real-Estate SEO & Bilingual Copywriting Skill

Use this skill when transforming raw property specs into compelling, localized rental listings and social media marketing copy for Dhaka City.

## 1. Core Copywriting Principles
- **Lead with the Hook**: Mention facing/orientation, key landmark, or category advantage immediately (e.g., *"South-Facing 3-Bed Lakeview Flat in Dhanmondi"* or *"Ready IT Office near Banani Road 11"*).
- **Zero Spec Fabrication**: Only advertise amenities actually present in the listing (do not invent "Titas gas" or "2 parking slots").
- **Standardized Formatting**: Always express rent in **BDT (`৳`)**, size in **`sqft`**, and clearly state **Advance Months**.

## 2. Neighborhood Positioning Hooks
- **Gulshan**: Diplomatic zone proximity, Gulshan Lake, circle access, 24/7 corporate security, full generator backup.
- **Banani**: Road 11 dining/commercial hub, Kemal Ataturk Avenue, boutique offices, airport road connectivity.
- **Dhanmondi**: Dhanmondi Lake, top schools/universities/hospitals, quiet residential streets, family-friendly.
- **Mirpur**: **Dhaka Metro Rail (MRT-6)** station proximity, affordable family flats, bustling retail complexes.
- **Uttara**: Planned sector layout, Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport proximity, wide roads, parks.
- **Motijheel**: Central Financial & Banking District, Dilkusha/Shapla Chattar corporate prestige, Metro Rail Motijheel terminal.

## 3. Channel-Specific Output Formats

### Channel A: Facebook To-Let / Rental Group Post (English)
- **Style**: Engaging, emoji-anchored scannable blocks.
- **Structure**:
  1. Attention-grabbing header with area & category (`🏠 TO-LET | ...`)
  2. Quick highlight bullets (Size, Floor, Rent in `৳`, Advance)
  3. Amenities & location hook
  4. Clear Call-to-Action (Phone number / Schedule visit)

### Channel B: Bikroy / Classified Portal Listing
- **Style**: Clean, factual, keyword-rich for search filters.
- **Structure**:
  1. SEO Title (`[Size] sqft [Category] for Rent in [Area], Dhaka`)
  2. Structured specification list
  3. Concise neighborhood description

### Channel C: Bangla Local Ad (বাংলা বিজ্ঞাপন)
- **Style**: Natural, respectful, authentic Bangladeshi real-estate Bengali.
- **Key Vocabulary**:
  - Apartment/Flat: `ফ্ল্যাট ভাড়া হবে` / `বাসা ভাড়া`
  - Office/Shop: `অফিস স্পেস ভাড়া` / `দোকান বা শোরুম ভাড়া`
  - Rent & Advance: `মাসিক ভাড়া: ৳ ...` | `অগ্রিম: ... মাস`
  - Amenities: `লিফট`, `সার্বক্ষণিক জেনারেটর`, `সিসিটিভি নিরাপত্তা`, `গ্যাস সংযোগ`, `পার্কিং সুবিধা`
  - Contact: `যোগাযোগ:`
